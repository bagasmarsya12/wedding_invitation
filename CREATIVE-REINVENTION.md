# The Garden Between Pages

## What stays

The envelope and continuous opening sequence, supplied B–I monogram, actual event information, personal invitation tokens, persisted RSVP, gifts/reservations, moderated marks, public/private boundaries, and server-owned admin controls.

## The problem

The previous interior repeated the same light, title-and-card rhythm. Large photo placeholders dominated the composition. More independently moving flowers would amplify decoration without changing the experience.

## The concept

A paper invitation opens into a small botanical room. The formal information stays direct. After RSVP the room deepens into a dark garden passage, then returns to quieter personal material. Archive entries are opened like drawers rather than browsed as a conventional grid.

## Main changes

1. Architectural hero: arched recess, layered supplied Combretum, translucent plane, paper sill and adjustable light. Date, venue, times, Maps and RSVP stay visible. The envelope also shows essential event information.
2. A garden passage: sticky scene with scroll-linked branch movement, restrained depth, dark/light contrast, and a clear route to the couple. No scroll hijacking or required waiting.
3. Cabinet Archive: native details drawers, real published records, original mark, preserved filters and direct story links. A graceful empty collection is preferable to fabricated evidence.
4. Supporting rooms: quieter photo mounts, an orchid date folio, gift shelving, tactile guest postcards, and clearer type rhythm.

## Wild idea carried into the build

The same climber appears at different depths: behind a transparent hero plane, framing the dark passage, then at the Archive entrance. This is a visual callback, not a claim that disconnected sprites form one physically continuous plant. Its movement remains anchored at the branch origin.

## Motion and accessibility

No WebGL dependency or audio was introduced. CSS layers and a small on-demand requestAnimationFrame controller are sufficient. Scroll is native; frames settle and stop; offscreen scenes are not measured; hidden tabs stop; touch devices do not use pointer parallax. Reduced motion removes entrance and scroll movement. Native range, details, links and existing forms remain keyboard-operable. No-JavaScript readers can still read the invitation and use Maps.

## Asset provenance

All new images are mechanical cutouts of the user-supplied Combretum sheet. Original artwork remains in the private workspace resource library; only selected optimized copies are served. Eleven cutouts were produced; large compositions use five. No new external imagery, fictional people, handwriting or memories.

## Content boundary

Profile photos and extended personal stories remain honest placeholders until supplied. Public marks still require approval and public visibility. Gifts require a personal invitation; no recipient details appear on the public landing page. Existing event details are not inferred from moodboard examples.
